# data/mock/ — RouteWise Agentic

> **Owner:** Shared — curated by Workstream A to back its tools; realistic Sri Lankan fixtures.
> **Layer:** mock data.
> Small, curated mock fixtures that make the MVP demo **reliable and honest**.

## What goes here (added in A4/A7)

- Candidate routes, plausible **LKR** fares, believable delays, and availability states — each
  labeled `mock` / `simulated`.

## Boundaries

- Committed **on purpose** (small + intentional). Real GTFS/GTFS-RT and PostGIS-backed transit
  data are **Workstream B** and live in the gitignored `data/raw/` and `data/gtfs/`.
- Never present mock data as real-time (see [`AGENT_SPEC.md` §15](../../docs/AGENT_SPEC.md)).
